        Strain

        What is strain?

        How much load your body has taken on today, from 0 to 21. It builds from your heart rate: workouts, but also walking, stress and everyday movement.

        The scale is logarithmic, so 18 to 21 takes far more effort than 8 to 11.

          HRV

          What is HRV?

          Heart rate variability is the tiny change in time between one heartbeat and the next, in milliseconds, measured while you sleep.

          Higher usually means your body is recovered and ready. Lower can mean stress, poor sleep, illness or hard training. It's very personal, so compare it with your own average, not with other people.

              Resting heart rate

              Resting heart rate

              How fast your heart beats when you're at rest, in beats per minute, measured while you sleep.

              A lower number usually means a fitter, more recovered heart. A rise of several beats above your own average can be a sign of stress, illness, alcohol, dehydration or poor sleep.

                Vitals

                What are these?

                Sleep efficiency: the share of your time in bed that you were actually asleep. Above 85% is good.

                Blood oxygen: how much oxygen your blood carries overnight. 95–100% is normal.

                Breathing: breaths per minute while you sleep. 12–20 is typical.

                Weight: your latest weight on record.
